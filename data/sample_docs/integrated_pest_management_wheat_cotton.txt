Title: Integrated Pest and Disease Management in Wheat, Cotton, and Pulses
Category: Pest & Disease Protection Guide
Crop: Wheat, Cotton, Chickpea
Geography: Northern & Central India
Year: 2024
Language: English

1. WHEAT IPM STRATEGIES
- Yellow Rust (Puccinia striiformis): Characterized by linear yellow pustules along leaf veins forming stripes. Most severe in cool, humid sub-montane conditions. Economic threshold level (ETL): 1-2 rusted leaves per square meter. Chemical intervention: Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 1 ml/L or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1.25 ml/L on initial symptom appearance.
- Karnal Bunt (Tilletia indica): Infected grains converted into black powdery mass with fishy odor (trimethylamine). Sowing before Nov 25 and avoiding excessive late irrigations during flowering reduces incidence.
- Aphids (Rhopalosiphum padi): Suck sap from leaves and earheads. ETL: 5 aphids per earhead. Spray Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.25 g/L or Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 ml/L.

2. COTTON INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT
- Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella): Larvae bore into bolls, creating rosette flowers and stained lint. ETL: 8 moths per pheromone trap per night for 3 consecutive nights, or 10% damaged green bolls. Biological management: Install 5 pheromone traps/acre and release Trichogramma bactrae @ 60,000 parasitized eggs/acre weekly. Chemical control: Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Profenofos 50% EC @ 2 ml/L.
- Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci): Vector for Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV). ETL: 6-8 nymphs/adults per leaf. Spray Pyriproxyfen 10% EC @ 2 ml/L or Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.2 g/L.

3. CHICKPEA (GRAM) POD BORER MANAGEMENT
- Gram Pod Borer (Helicoverpa armigera): Larvae feed on leaves and bore into developing pods with body halfway outside. ETL: 1-2 larvae per meter row length.
- Intercrop with Coriander or Mustard as trap crop; install bird perches @ 20/acre.
- Bio-pesticides: Spray NPV (Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus) @ 250 LE/acre or Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) @ 1.5 g/L.
- Chemical control: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (Coragen) @ 0.3 ml/L or Indoxacarb 14.5% SC @ 1 ml/L.
